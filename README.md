# Machine-Learning-For-Predicting-Renewable-Energy-Output

The work aims to develop a predictive model to forecast energy output from renewable sources like solar panels, wind turbines etc. based on the historical data and environmental factors. The “Global Renewable Energy Consumption” dataset is used here. The dataset includes hourly energy output data from various renewable energy sources along with weather conditions and other environmental factors. This dataset is sourced from energy companies and public databases maintained by energy regulatory bodies.
